About the Author

I don't know if "author" is the proper term for me in this context. While I've written the content on this site, I'm not the original creator of the story of the Endurance expedition. My goal is to share the incredible tale of the crew of Endurance, and to honor their legacy.

In university I studied film and screenwriting, and in 2025 I was looking for my next story to write. I wanted to write a non-fiction screenplay focusing on an event nobody—or at least few people—had set to screen. And so I chose the story of the Endurance expedition. In collating data and events from various sources, I realized I needed a place to store and organize all the information I was gathering. Then I had the idea to focus on events day-by-day. This website is the result of that idea.

Origins of the Endurance Project

I first learned about about the Imperial Trans-Atlantic Expedition (ITAE) by going down the Wikipedia rabbit hole reading about survival stories sometime in the early 2020s. But something about the ITAE stuck with me—its themes of endurance, leadership, and camaraderie in the face of overwhelming odds. One of the things that most interested me was the fact that all 28 crewmen survived. It almost seems impossible that more than two dozen men could survive two years in one of if not the most inhospitable places on Earth, but they did.

I wanted to learn more about how they managed to do it, and in June of 2025 I bought my first book on the subject, Endurance: Shackleton's Incredible Voyage by Alfred Lansing. Upon finishing it I was hooked on the story, and I needed to know more. I read South, Sir Ernest Shackleton's own account of the expedition, and Shackleton: An Irishman in Antarctica, a detailed biography of Shackleton by Jonathan Shackleton and John MacKenna. I also read several diaries from several crewmen.

After months of research, I decided to start writing a screenplay about the expedition. But, I wanted to organize all the information I had gathered in a way that made sense to me. I initially planned to create daily logs of the day-by-day events of the Weddell Sea Party, but as I started writing I realized that putting this information together could be interesting to others. That's how this website was born. I hope you find it as fascinating as I do.
