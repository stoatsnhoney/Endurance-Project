*Endurance* anchors in Madeira, Portugal. [Captain Frank Worsley](/worsley) and [Thomas Orde-Lees](/orde-lees) stay with Mr. E Blandy at Palheiro.

In the afternoon, the SS *Hochfeld* anchors too close to *Endurance*. At 10 p.m. it hits *Endurance*, causing damage to the jibboom.

[Vincent](/vincent), [McCarthy](/mccarthy), O. Barr, and J. Irving, apparently drunk, start a small riot in a café.

**Note:** I could note find any record of *Endurance* stopping in Portugal. However, Worsley's log references the above events. He mentions the Blandy Bros company which operated in Madeira, Portugal, which Palheiro is also near.
