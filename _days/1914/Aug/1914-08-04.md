---
date: 1914-08-04
years: 1914-08
---
In the morning, Shackleton went ashore and read in the paper about the order for general mobilization. He returned to the ship and proposed—to the agreement off all hands—send a telegram to the admiralty offering the services of *Endurance* and its crew in the war effort. Within an hour of sending it, the crew received a single-word response from the Admiralty: "Proceed."

A few hours later, Winston Churchill sent a longer telegram that the authorities alongside the Scientific and Geographical societies wished the expedition to proceed. *Endurance* set sail for Plymouth.
